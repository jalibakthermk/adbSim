Headlamps · ADB / matrix beam · glare

adbSim ADB matrix beam simulator v0.5.1

Segment-by-segment control of a matrix headlamp around other road users: beam view, glare at target points, road view, timeline and kept illumination.

Files are processed locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Project and export

A project (*.adb.json) embeds the IES files, scene, controller, limits and scenario, so it reproduces the run. The Road-illuminate scenario carries the beams, segment states and targets (one frame, or the timeline when targets move) for its road view.

Photometry

Files are numbered in natural name order. Segments are drawn left to right by their centre.

Negative H is left. Each segment is the high beam masked between two boundaries.

Targets

Straight road. Lane 1 is your own lane (own direction) or the lane next to the centre line (oncoming). d is to the front of an oncoming target and to the rear of a preceding one.

ADB controller

Dynamic scenario

d and v in the target list are at t = 0. Oncoming targets close at ego + target speed; preceding ones move at target − ego speed. The controller sees each target's position from t − latency.

Vehicle and road

Origin on the road under the midpoint between the lamps. Aim applies to both lamps.

Limits

Loaded from data/limits.json. The shipped file has no values (null = no limit): fill them in from the regulation or customer specification.

Timeline

Strips: one row per segment (1 at the top), filled = on, empty = off. The slider drives the beam view, road view, glare table and metrics.
